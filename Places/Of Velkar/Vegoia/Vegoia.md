Vegoia is a land ruled by magic. It fuel the industry and economy, developing new technologies every year. The city has trams spreading across it, greatly helping with transportation and locomotion. The city house many laboratories and archives in its streets. They call their magical devices *Magicasters*. It seems like the devices are capable of using magic, but they need to be powered externally. Magicasters are very common in Vegoia, ranging from lamps, earphones, communication devices (WitText), Magifreezer, Flarepot (magic stove), clocks, and so on. Magic is also a sign of social status, indicated by the color of your cape among it. The lighter it is in color, the higher your magic capacity and the better your rank. Magic was first introduced by Desiree, when they planted the Divine Tree (a pomegranate tree) at the heart of the Academy. Also, one’s magic capacity does not increase, it instead stay unchanged over time. – CN 7/27/2024
