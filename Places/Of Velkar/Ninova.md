Now, only a crater remain, the Ninova Crater, where outlaws, criminals and those in exile gather. This crater is located in the center of Velkar. Together they created a city ruled by four houses. We will have to infiltrate one of the house to look for Finnion and Nayan. – CN 10/19/2024
