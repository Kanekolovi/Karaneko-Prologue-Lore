The last sequence of the game without branching into different endings is at that point the last occasion to choose your ending (going dagger?, going neutral?, and bad wolf ending)

During this last day, the visitors are gone and you are presented with multiple dialog choices as Yurim is slowly going through a loneliness breakdown. Yesterday’s dispute still lingers heavily on her mind. She tries to forget and pass on, but even though we insist multiple times on finding Nayan or wanting to leave.

She desperately tries her best to stay cheerful, to keep a face that will make Karam stay, but we have to leave. But she apparently can’t by herself, so gets really sad and mad that we would have to leave her. She would be alone one more time, who knows how long for.

When crying in her house, she reveals that she knew the people here were fake and confesses her love to Karam

She also says that “Look, I made everything Pink”, “How much more pink do I have to make it for you to stay!?”

Lastly, she hardens her resolve and tries to summon a sort of black hole, possibly with the intent to destroy this whole place. Something that she “should have done to make Finnion stay back then”
But can’t. Probably physically.
Although she can’t achieve her full goal, that display of power still has absorbed all the liveliness of the forest: NPCs are either absent or intangible ghosts, and the place is silent and spooky.
