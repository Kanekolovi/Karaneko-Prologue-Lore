A dark, pointy dagger given by Oskos that is used in combats to quickly shoot small projectiles in front of you, like you would usually in a bullet hell game. 

Random before I forget
- We are warned a few times by Rosemyne about what Oskos gives. One time in the Cabin.
- Really _**kills**_ the foes defeated by it; they fade away and cannot be encountered anymore.
- While it is advertised to change the patterns/difficulty of some fights (making them supposedly “harder”) [^1], the only fight it was found to actually influence is of the xylophone moth.
- The wolf and Oskos are unaffected by its “killing” power.
