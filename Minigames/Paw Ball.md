A two player soccer game that you play with Yurim at her house:

First to 3 goals wins a round, first to three rounds is the winner! The loser gets to cook food for the evening.

The game is a simple “Push the ball in the opponent’s goal” game, the AI doesn’t change when Yurim is playing or not: it aims to place itself diagonally above the ball and then juggles into the ball until the ai is below, which happens often after hitting a wall or the player steals the ball. Notably: it can score against itself, much like a human player.
